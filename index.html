Chapter 4: Story & Moral Lesson

Date: February 12, 2020

Title: Go And Go Until You Die

From the creations of Death Comes Alive And "The Rages Band"

Once upon a time, two kids played near the backyard. Step Toe parents disciplined them, warning: "Always know the steps you will walk on your foot and also seeing around the holes... Don't step on it. Or you will 'Go And Go Until You Die'!"

When the parents left for Jakarta for work for 1 month, the spoiled kids disrespected the warnings and stepped into deep pit holes for fun. They fell into a massive dumpsite hole and perished.

Moral Lesson

Always take care of your life as parents advise. If second chances are gone, life cannot be given back for good. Respect the path you walk!

YOU FELL INTO THE HOLE!

You disrespected the parental warning and stepped directly into the dark dumpsite hole. Remember: life doesn't give a second chance once it's over!
